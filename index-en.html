SRT to Sound

Create synchronized Russian narration from finished SRT subtitles as M4A or WAV.

Release · 3.5.0
Download for macOSUser guide ↗

Before you startInstall Python 3.12 ARM64, FFmpeg, Silero packages and v5_5_ru.pt separately; they are not bundled. Not notarized by Apple.

How it works

  1. Open SRT
  2. Synthesize speech
  3. Save M4A / WAV

First steps

Extract the app and complete dependency setup in the guide first. Add .ru.srt files and choose voice, format and output folder.
